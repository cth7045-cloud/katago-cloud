문제 은행(problems/)의 문제 출처 / Sources of the problems in this folder

1. OGS (online-go.com) puzzles — classical collections (발양론, 기경중묘, 현람, 사활묘기) entered by OGS members, read from the public API
   https://online-go.com/api/v1/puzzles/collections/<id>/puzzles . Problem id "o<number>" = OGS puzzle <number>
   (https://online-go.com/puzzle/<number>).

2. Classical collections (관자보 Guan Zi Pu, 현현기경 Xuan Xuan Qi Jing, 기경중묘 Qi Jing Zhong Miao) from Ulrich Görtz's
   "Classical go problems", https://www.u-go.net/classic/ — prepared from the Flygo files (http://www.flygo.net/) with their
   permission to make them available; 현현기경 compiled by Jean-Pierre Vesinet. Problem ids "u…".

3. 망우청락집 Wangyou Qingle Ji: the SGF files in https://github.com/tasuki/tsumego (books/sources/wangyou-qingle-ji).

4. Problem ids "q…" (problems added to 현현기경·사활묘기·관자보·기경중묘·발양론) and the answer lines of
   the 답지 positions (DB bank_edits, note "원서 답지:"): a 고전 사활 전집 PDF supplied by the site's operator (2026-11-05) —
   positions read from its diagrams, the main line from its 정해 수순; no wrong lines. (천룡도 Tianlong Tu, also in that PDF, is a modern book by Kweon Kab-yong — not used.)

5. 셰이프 업! (shelf 모양·행마, DB book x5ba9e1, problem ids "u…", 2026-11-07): the three problem sets of
   "Shape Up!" by Seong-June Kim (김성준) and Charles Matthews, https://cdn.online-go.com/shape_up.pdf
   (2015 repackaging by Akita Noek, 2019 edition by Brian Dunn), licensed under Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0,
   https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/ . Changes: the problem and answer diagrams were turned into
   interactive problems (stones placed on a 19×19 board by the diagram's edges and star points; right line and the
   answer to the wrong choice from the book's answer diagrams; five of those answers, where the book gives none, chosen
   with KataGo or set to the right point). These adapted problems are shared under the same license, CC BY-SA 4.0.
   The same book's explanatory diagrams (554 studies in 학습방 › 연구, 2026-11-07) are adapted too: diagrams turned into
   SGF records (moves and variations from the numbered stones) and the explanations translated into Korean; each
   record carries this attribution, and they are shared under CC BY-SA 4.0 as well.

Since 2026-11-05 only classical collections (published before 1911) are kept here; the modern books were removed —
except openly licensed material such as 5.
