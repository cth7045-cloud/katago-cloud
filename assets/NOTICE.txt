KataGoVast web analysis screen - third-party material

The analysis screen (remote/webui.html) reproduces the look of LizzieYzy Next
(board texture, stones, background, candidate-move drawing rules, win-rate
panels) so it looks the same as the desktop program.

* board.png, black0.png, white0.png, background.jpg
  From LizzieYzy Next (lizzie-yzy2.5.3-shaded.jar, assets/), https://github.com/wimi321/lizzieyzy-next
  Licensed under the GNU General Public License v3.0.
* The drawing rules in webui.html (margins, line widths, candidate colors and
  text layout, stone shadows/highlights, win-rate bar, graph, variation tree)
  were ported from LizzieYzy Next's BoardRenderer.java, GlassEffectRenderer.java,
  LizzieFrame.java, WinrateGraph.java and VariationTree.java (GPL-3.0).
  Therefore remote/webui.html is distributed under the GNU GPL v3.0 as well.
  The full license text is in LICENSE-GPL-3.0.txt next to this file.
* OpenSans-Semibold.ttf
  Open Sans, Copyright Google Inc., Apache License 2.0
  (bundled with LizzieYzy Next as its win-rate font).
